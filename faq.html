Orders & Delivery

How do I place an order on Quantum Jersey?

You can order through our website or directly on WhatsApp. Select your product and size, then use the available WhatsApp or cart checkout options to submit your order. Our team will confirm the order with you on WhatsApp before payment.

How much is delivery?

Standard nationwide delivery is ₦5,000 per order.

Is the ₦5,000 delivery fee charged per item?

No. The standard ₦5,000 delivery fee is charged once per order, regardless of the number of items, quantities, or variants in that order.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery typically takes 1–3 business days depending on your location.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Yes. Same-day delivery is available in Lagos only. Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm availability and arrangements for your location.

Do you deliver outside Nigeria?

Yes. International delivery is available, with shipping arrangements and pricing handled case-by-case through WhatsApp.

Are there additional delivery charges for remote locations?

Some remote or out-of-coverage locations may attract an additional courier or waybill surcharge. If applicable, we will confirm the additional charge with you on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Yes. You can request changes to your size, quantity, address, or other order details before dispatch.

If a change or second delivery is required after dispatch because of a mistake on the customer's end, the customer is responsible for the second delivery cost.

What are your business hours?

Our business and WhatsApp support hours are:

  • Monday–Saturday: 8:00 AM–6:00 PM WAT
  • Sunday: 12:00 PM–6:00 PM WAT

Products & Sizing

How do I choose the right size?

Each product page includes a size guide. As a general guide, if you're between sizes and prefer a relaxed fit, size up. If you're still unsure, message us with your measurements and we'll help you choose the right size.

What sizes are available?

Sizes vary by product. Football boots currently use EU sizing, while jerseys and sportswear use the sizes shown on each product page.

For jerseys, player versions commonly use M, L, XL, and 2XL.

Children's jerseys generally follow:

  • 1–2 years: Size 16
  • 3–4 years: Size 18
  • 5–6 years: Size 20
  • 7–8 years: Size 22
  • 9–10 years: Size 24
  • 11–12 years: Size 26
  • 12–13 years: Size 28 / 30

Older teenagers may be able to wear player-version M depending on their measurements and preferred fit. Always check the individual product's size guide or contact us on WhatsApp if unsure.

What if my preferred size is unavailable?

Message us on WhatsApp with the product and size you're looking for. We'll check available stock and let you know if we can source it for you.

Do you offer custom name and number printing?

Yes. Custom name and number printing is available on jerseys. Contact us on WhatsApp with your requested name and squad number to arrange customization.

What type of products do you sell?

Quantum Jersey offers football boots, jerseys, and sportswear.

How would you describe your products?

We offer premium-quality football and sportswear products selected for customers who want quality gear at competitive Nigerian-market prices.

Returns & Exchanges

Can I exchange an item if the size doesn't fit?

Yes. Size exchanges are available within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock availability.

If your preferred replacement size is unavailable, we'll offer a full refund instead.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Unworn, unaltered items with all original tags attached can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

Items must be in the same condition in which they were received.

Are personalised jerseys returnable?

No. Personalised/name-printed jerseys are non-returnable.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us immediately with photos of the damaged item and your order number. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

What if my order is significantly delayed?

Contact us immediately with your order number. We'll review the delivery issue and arrange an appropriate resolution, including a replacement or full refund where applicable.
