You can order through our website or directly via WhatsApp:

Every order generated through our system receives a unique official Order Reference (e.g., QJ-202610-XXXX) for tracking and confirmation.

Standard nationwide delivery costs ₦5,000 per order across Nigeria. This flat fee applies once per order regardless of cart quantity. Remote destination surcharges may apply.

Estimated delivery is 1 to 3 business days, subject to courier transit schedules and destination confirmation. Orders are packaged in secure protective bags and dispatched promptly once payment has been verified.

Standard nationwide delivery costs ₦5,000 per order. Remote destination surcharges may apply for distant or out-of-coverage destinations.

How does order confirmation and tracking work?

Here is how your order is confirmed and tracked:

  • Order Reference: When your order is placed, you receive a unique Order Reference (e.g. QJ-XXXXXX-XXXX).

Yes. You can request changes to your size, delivery address, or quantity before dispatch by messaging our WhatsApp support line with your Order Reference. Once an order has been dispatched with the courier, rerouting may incur an additional delivery charge.

Products & Sizing

How do I select the right jersey size (Fan vs. Player Version)?

We offer both Fan Version and Player Version jerseys with distinct fits:

  • Fan Version: Designed for everyday supporter comfort with a standard, regular cut. Choose your usual casual shirt size.
  • Player Version: Engineered with lightweight, breathable performance fabric and an athletic, body-contouring slim fit (the exact cut worn by athletes on the pitch). If you prefer a looser or standard fit, we recommend ordering one size up.

Player version sizing typically spans M, L, XL, and 2XL. If you are uncertain about sizing, message us on WhatsApp with your measurements and we will help you choose.

How do I select the correct football boot size?

All football boots at Quantum Jersey use standard European (EU) sizing (e.g., EU 40, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45).

If you wear thick football grip socks or have wider feet, we suggest going half or one size up for optimal comfort and ball control.

What sizes are available for kids' jerseys?

Children's jerseys follow these age-guided size numbers:

  • 1–2 years: Size 16
  • 3–4 years: Size 18
  • 5–6 years: Size 20
  • 7–8 years: Size 22
  • 9–10 years: Size 24
  • 11–12 years: Size 26
  • 12–13 years: Size 28 / 30

Older teenagers can typically wear Adult Fan or Player Version M depending on height and chest measurements.

Do you offer custom name and number printing?

Yes. Custom name and squad number printing is available on football jerseys. Message us on WhatsApp with your requested player name, number, and badge preference to arrange customization before dispatch.

Important: Personalised or customised jerseys are not eligible for returns or exchanges.

What type of gear does Quantum Jersey sell?

Quantum Jersey specializes in premium-quality football boots, club & national team jerseys (both Fan and Player editions), and performance sportswear curated for Nigerian ballers and supporters.
